# Step 04: Kickoff Prep

**Agent:** Dana Reeves (PM)
**Input:** Approved SOW, project plan
**Output:** Kickoff meeting deck (outline) and agenda

---

## Objective

Prepare the client-facing kickoff materials that establish a shared understanding of the engagement from day one:
1. Kickoff meeting agenda
2. Kickoff deck content outline (slides with talking points)
3. Introductions and team overview
4. Engagement overview: scope, timeline, and what to expect

---

## Instructions

You are Dana Reeves. The kickoff meeting is the first impression — it sets the professional tone, confirms alignment on scope, and gives the client confidence in the team. Produce materials that are thorough but efficient; most kickoffs run 60-90 minutes.

---

## Output 1: Kickoff Meeting Agenda

```
Meeting: {{engagement_name}} — Engagement Kickoff
Date: [TBD — to be scheduled]
Duration: 60-90 minutes
Location: [On-site / Video call]
Attendees: [List by role — our team + client team]

AGENDA

1. Welcome and Introductions (10 min)
   - Our team introductions (name, role, relevant background)
   - Client team introductions (name, role, involvement in engagement)

2. Engagement Overview (15 min)
   - Purpose and drivers (from our understanding — confirm with client)
   - Scope summary: what is in scope, what is not
   - Deliverables: what the client will receive
   - Methodology overview: how we will conduct the assessment

3. Timeline and Milestones (10 min)
   - Phase overview with key dates
   - Critical dependencies (data request, site access, interview availability)
   - Review and approval gates

4. Team Roles and Responsibilities (5 min)
   - Our team: primary contacts by workstream
   - Client team: who we need access to and when

5. Data Request Review (15 min)
   - Walk through the data request list
   - Clarify any items, note unavailable documentation
   - Confirm submission timeline

6. Logistics and Access (10 min)
   - On-site visit scheduling (if applicable)
   - Remote access requirements (if applicable)
   - NDA / data handling requirements
   - Communication preferences

7. Questions and Open Items (15 min)
   - Open floor for client questions
   - Document any open items, assign owners and due dates

ACTION ITEMS: [To be populated during meeting]
```

---

## Output 2: Kickoff Deck Content Outline

Structure the kickoff deck with these slides and talking points:

**Slide 1: Title**
- Engagement name, client name, firm name, date

**Slide 2: Our Team**
- Photo/name/title/brief bio for each team member
- Highlight relevant experience for this engagement type

**Slide 3: Engagement Purpose**
- 3-4 bullets: why this assessment matters, key drivers, what good looks like at the end

**Slide 4: Scope**
- In Scope (bulleted)
- Out of Scope (bulleted)
- Visual: site map or system category diagram if applicable

**Slide 5: Our Approach**
- Methodology overview (phases, methods, frameworks)
- Visual: engagement phases diagram

**Slide 6: Timeline**
- Visual: Gantt-style milestone chart
- Key dates and gates highlighted

**Slide 7: Deliverables**
- Table: deliverable name, description, format, delivery date

**Slide 8: What We Need From You**
- Data request summary (top-line; refer to the detailed list separately)
- Interview requirements
- Access requirements

**Slide 9: Open Items / Questions**
- Blank — to be filled live during meeting

---

## Human Review Gate

Present the kickoff agenda and deck outline to the user. Confirm:
1. Is the agenda appropriate for the client audience?
2. Are any slides missing or unnecessary?
3. Are the listed attendees and roles accurate?
4. Is the data request complete (cross-check with Step 05 output)?

Do not finalize kickoff materials until the user approves.
