## Purpose

This document records every material assumption on which this engagement's scope, timeline, pricing, or technical analysis is based. Assumptions are not excuses — they are explicit conditions that must be validated before or during the engagement.

If an assumption is wrong, that is a trigger for a scope change conversation with the client, not silent absorption of additional work.

---

## Assumption Categories

- **Technical** — about the client's environment, systems, or maturity
- **Resource** — about staff availability, interview access, or documentation readiness
- **Timeline** — about client response times, scheduling, and milestone flexibility
- **Regulatory** — about applicable compliance requirements
- **Commercial** — about pricing, change orders, or engagement structure

## Field Definitions

**Assumption:** A complete, falsifiable statement. Not "client documentation is available" — but "client will provide all items on the data request list within 5 business days of receiving it." A good assumption can be proven true or false.

**Basis:** Where did this assumption come from? Proposal language, client statement during discovery, standard practice, or inferred from engagement type?

**Owner:** Who is responsible for validating this assumption? (Client contact, PM, BD, Assessor)

**Risk If Wrong:** What happens to scope, timeline, or pricing if this assumption doesn't hold?
- Low: Minor inconvenience, absorbable within engagement
- Medium: Scope discussion required, timeline may slip
- High: Scope change order required; significant additional hours

**Validated:** Updated during the engagement as each assumption is confirmed or challenged.
